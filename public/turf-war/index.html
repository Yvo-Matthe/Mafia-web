Turf War

A real-time mafia strategy game

Three rival families want your city. Take it from them.

  • Earn: every block you control pays you cash every second. Open a front business on a block to double its take.
  • Recruit: hire Thugs, Enforcers and Hitmen at your headquarters (bottom-left).
  • Select: click a unit, or drag a box. Q selects your whole crew.
  • Move: right-click (or tap, on touch screens) to send the selection. They fight anything on the way.
  • Capture: stand in a block with no enemies around to take it over.
  • Win: take every rival headquarters, or control 18 of the 24 blocks. Lose your own HQ and it's over.
